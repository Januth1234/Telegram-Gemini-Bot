Pricing

Orin AI offers a free plan and paid Basic and Pro plans. Prices in LKR (Sri Lankan Rupees).

Plans

  • Free – For casual exploration. Daily reset limit, standard speed, community support.
  • Basic – 300 LKR/month or 3,000 LKR/year. Expanded limits, priority access, cloud sync.
  • Pro – 1,500 LKR/month or 15,000 LKR/year. For creators and students. Expanded limits, priority access, Logic Flow, Voice Mode.
